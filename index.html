    How to play

    The goal

    Spell all thirteen coffee words on the board at the same time. A word counts when its letters sit side by side in a row, reading left to right, or in a column, reading top to bottom. Words can go anywhere, cross each other, and share letters.

    The catch

    There are sixty tiles and only four empty spaces, and you can move one tile at a time. Getting three or four words is easy. Keeping them while you build the rest is the puzzle.

    Nothing locks

    A spelled word glows gold and gets checked off. Slide a letter out and it un-spells. Every arrangement that shows all thirteen words wins, so there are many solutions, and none of them are handed to you.

    Play it fast

    Steer with the arrow keys, hop between the four empty spaces with Tab or 1 to 4, and undo with Z. Hands never leave the keyboard.

    Rules

    • Slide one tile at a time into an empty space.
    • Spell every word in the list at once, left to right or top to bottom.
    • Words may sit anywhere and may share letters.
    • Nothing locks. Moving a letter out un-spells the word.
    • Fewer moves and a faster time make a better score.
